spunto. Design mockups · v0.2 · 13 October 2026

The core screens of the Android app (a 360 dp phone) and the web app (a 1200 px window), drawn only from the tokens in docs/03-design.md §11, in the default Carta theme. Sample notes are invented but realistic. The instance shown has a few plugins on, and frames show their output where it lands: label suggestions and the Review line, Related in the note panel, Ask, the From agents and Tasks views, and the daily slip. Every other plugin, the plugin manager and the three themes are in redesign.html.

AndroidWebTokens
  • + idea dashed means proposed by a plugin, not yet yours
  • Aa serif is your words
  • Aa sans is the app talking

Android

Native Compose with Material 3 structure (sheets, springs, back gesture) dressed in the app's own paper, ink and type. One main screen; labels and search are sheets and modes of it.

Web

A keyboard-first desktop tool: a collapsible label rail, one centred column at reading measure with hanging timestamps, and a single palette for search, labels, actions and asking.

Tokens

The same values as 03-design.md §11, in Carta, the default theme. Contrast figures are WCAG ratios against paper; every text token clears 4.5:1 in light and dark. Ardesia and Salvia, with their full contrast tables, are in redesign.html.
